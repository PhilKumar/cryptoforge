The V-RuleV-Rule

The rules it ranஇயங்கிய விதிகள்

The VV அமைப்புA dip below the standing mother, two green candles since that dip, and the first red confirms it.
EntryநுழைவுA line a quarter of the way from the lowest low back to the mother high; it trails the low down.
SizingஅளவுThe pot is the fall percentage of the purse, split 30% then 70%, two bands — four buys, then it holds.
Fee gateகட்டண வாசல்A buy waits until the expected win clears 0.35% of price, so a win cannot be a donation.
BudgetநிதிNever more than half the purse committed at once; profit folds in at 25%.
Targetஇலக்குAverage buy plus a quarter of the fall, the whole ladder at once, net of 0.1% a side.

What this does not showஇது காட்டாதவை

Every closed round is a winner by constructionமுடிந்த ஒவ்வொரு சுற்றும் அமைப்பாலேயே வெற்றி

There is no stop-loss anywhere in this family. A round closes when its target is reached, so the 10,459 closed rounds contain 0 losses. The risk did not disappear — it is sitting in the open bag below.

The bag is the whole riskகைவசம் இருப்பதே முழு ஆபத்து

52 ladders were still holding $2,407 of coin when the window ended. They are valued here at the last close, not at what they might fetch.

Money is held for a long timeபணம் நீண்ட காலம் பிடிக்கப்படுகிறது

The longest single hold ran 1,351 days. A book like this is not a stream of income; it is capital committed and waited on.

The purse grows only on its own profitபணப்பை தன் லாபத்தில் மட்டுமே வளர்கிறது

Each coin starts from $200 and is never topped up. Because the budget cap scales with a compounding purse, peak capital committed runs above the starting number — that is why every rate on this page is measured against peak capital used, not the nameplate.

One market, one venue, no slippage modelஒரே சந்தை, ஒரே இடம்

Binance spot 5-minute bars. Fills are candle-resolution: a stop fills when a bar's high reaches it, and a bar that took an entry is never also allowed to take the target. Fees are charged at 0.1% a side; spread and slippage are not modelled.
