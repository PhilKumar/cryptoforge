What this is not. No backtest is mixed in and no paper round is counted — a blended live-and-simulated figure cannot be checked by anyone, including us. The account was funded once with — and never topped up. The whole ledger, trade by trade with its fees, is behind the door.

How it thinks

Three verbs. Nothing else gets a vote.

The whole desk is a loop that watches, waits and writes. It has no view on where the market is going, and it is never allowed to develop one.

01

Watch

Every candle on every watched pair, all night, in the same four numbers. No headlines, no influencers, no gut. The desk reads price and nothing but price.

02

Wait

An order sits armed until price proves the rule — not a minute sooner. Most days it does nothing at all. That is not the desk being idle; that is the method working.

03

Write

Every fill lands in the journal with its fee attached, before anyone has read it. The losing days go in the same ink as the winning ones. Nothing is edited later.

What this page will not tell you

There is no return figure here. On purpose.

A percentage on a landing page, without its losing days beside it, is an advertisement. The journal carries both — every fill, every fee, every day it went the wrong way — and it is behind the door, where it can be read whole or not at all.

This is a working desk trading its owner’s own money, not a fund and not a course. Access is opened by hand, one conversation at a time.

  • ×No annual numberA single figure hides the sequence that made it. Read the sequence instead.
  • ×No backtest curveCurves drawn after the fact are cheap. Only live fills go in the journal.
  • ×No adviceNothing here is a recommendation to buy or sell anything, to anyone.
  • ✓Every losing day, publishedBecause the losing days are the only part you cannot fake.

Security

Real money, treated like it.

Keys stay on the desk

Exchange keys are stored encrypted, never shown again once entered, and never leave the box that trades with them. A viewer never sees them at all.

Two factors, then a third

Password, authenticator code, and a passkey where the device offers one. The owner’s door is no softer than anyone else’s.

A guard on the door

A deploy is refused while a trade is live, and a restart never wakes the engine on its own. Boring on purpose — the exciting failures have all been had once.
